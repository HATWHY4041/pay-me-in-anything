Exact amount, every time

Quotes are exact-output: you set $1, you receive 1.00 USDC. Swap costs are covered by the payer and shown before they send.

Overpayments go back

If a client sends a little extra, the difference is refunded to their own address automatically.

Wrong-network guard

Wallets list the same token on several chains. The pay page says exactly which network to send from, and has a Copy amount button so nobody rounds down.

Live status

The page updates on its own: waiting, received, converting, paid. No refreshing, no guessing.

Shareable receipts

Every payment gets a receipt link anyone can open to verify it live with NEAR Intents, with the NEAR transaction attached.

Questions

What does it cost?

Nothing to create a link. The payer covers the swap: in our tests that was about 1% on top of the invoice, including NEAR Intents' fee. They see the exact amount before they send, and you always receive the full amount.

What do I need to receive money?

Just a NEAR account, like yourname.near or the long address from a wallet such as HOT or Meteor. Payments arrive there as USDC.

Why is a chain or token greyed out?

NEAR Intents sometimes pauses a network (Ethereum mainnet is paused as of late October 2026), sets a temporary minimum (for example $100 for USDT on Tron), or has nobody offering to swap a token. Before your client picks anything, the pay page checks every token live for that exact invoice amount and only lets them choose ones that will go through. Greyed-out options show the reason, and come back by themselves when NEAR Intents lifts the limit.

What if my client sends the wrong amount or misses the deadline?

If they send extra, the difference is refunded to the address they gave. If they send too little, or the payment can't complete, NEAR Intents refunds them on their own chain. Nothing gets stuck.

How long does a payment take?

Our real mainnet test took 46 seconds from sending to paid. Bitcoin and other slower chains take longer while their network confirms the transaction.

Does Pay Me in Anything hold my money?

No. The app never has custody. Your client sends to a deposit address created by NEAR Intents for that one payment, and the USDC is delivered straight to your account.

Can my client pay from an exchange?

Sending straight from an exchange can be unreliable, because exchanges batch withdrawals and the refund address won't be theirs. A personal wallet is the safer choice.
